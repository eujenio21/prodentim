Everyday oral wellness
01 · The oral microbiome

A tiny ecosystem with a big role in oral science

The oral microbiome refers to the community of bacteria and other microorganisms that naturally live in the mouth. Many are part of a normal oral environment.

Researchers study how this community interacts with the mouth, the gums, saliva, diet and daily hygiene habits. The science is complex, and no single supplement replaces brushing, flossing or regular dental care.

02 · Everyday habits

Small routines remain the foundation of oral care

Products may attract attention, but consistent oral hygiene and professional guidance remain central to caring for your teeth and gums.

01

Brush consistently

Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste, following guidance from your dental professional.

02

Clean between teeth

Floss or use an appropriate interdental cleaner to reach areas a toothbrush may miss.

03

Keep dental visits

Regular dental checkups help you receive advice suited to your own oral health needs.

03 · Oral probiotics

Why are oral probiotics getting attention?

Probiotics are live microorganisms that, when used in adequate amounts, may provide a health benefit. Scientists are studying selected probiotic strains for possible roles in oral health.

Evidence varies by strain, dose, product and outcome. Findings from a study of one ingredient should not automatically be treated as proof that a finished supplement produces the same result.

If you are considering a supplement, review its full label and discuss questions with a qualified healthcare professional or dental professional.
